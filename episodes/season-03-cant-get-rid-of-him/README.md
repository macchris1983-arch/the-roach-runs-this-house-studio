## Premise

The Roach is a straight-up gangster from the Town who runs a full operation out of the couple's walls. He sells gas and crack crumbles, scams, steals and pulls licks, and he's almost impossible to get rid of.

The Husband and Wife have tried everything (sprays, traps, bug bombs, slippers) with no luck. This season they call in all the help they can get, and the Roach clowns every last one of them.

- **Format:** 7 episodes, about 35 seconds each. *(laugh)* marks a short audience laugh.
- **Tone:** raunchy roast battle with profanity and Bay Area slang. The Roach has the most disrespectful mouth in the house.
- **Rules:** no sex or nudity shown, and the kids are never around the drugs, the raunchy jokes or the cussing. They only show up in clean scenes, and nobody roasts them. The drug dealing stays a joke; nothing shows how to make or use anything.
- **The couple:** the Wife always has the Husband's back. They lose to the Roach together, never to each other.
- **Animation:** the room stays completely still; only the characters move (no camera zooms or push-ins).

## E01 — Everything But the Kitchen Sink

**Premise:** A rapid-fire montage of every way the couple has tried to kill the Roach. None of it works.

**Staging:** Kitchen. Quick cuts: slipper, spray can, sticky trap, bug bomb.

**Roach:** *(dodging a slipper)* Bruh, a house shoe? I done dodged raid cans with more heart than you, mane.

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** Baby, set off the bug bomb.

**Roach:** *(walking out the smoke in a gas mask)* Appreciate the hotbox, cuddy. That's hella gas.

*(laugh)*

**Husband:** Sticky trap. Brand new.

**Roach:** I don't step in traps. I SELL traps. Ask the mice, they owe me fetti.

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** Get the fuck out of my house!

**Roach:** Your house? Check the walls, lil mama. My name tagged all over 'em.

**Husband:** Brutus! Sic 'em!

*(The Rottweiler charges, growling. The Roach screams and scrambles on top of the fridge.)*

**Roach:** Aye aye AYE! Call off Cujo, bruh! I'm allergic to teeth!

*(laugh)*

**Cousin:** *(leaning in the window)* Brutus, sit.

*(Brutus sits.)*

**Cousin:** That's two crumbles, cuz.

*(The Roach tosses him a bag from the fridge top.)*

**Husband:** That's it. We calling in help.

**Roach:** Call the whole Town. On my mama, I ain't going nowhere.

*(laugh)*

**Loop:** The Roach counts a stack of cash inside the toaster while the couple stares.

## E02 — Tasha Tried It

**Premise:** The couple calls in a real exterminator. The Roach robs her truck and roasts her spray.

**Staging:** Living room. Tasha walks in silently with her sprayer. Through the window, the Cousin rolls her hubcaps down the street.

**Roach:** Ooh, they sent a professional? Lil mama, that spray expired in 2019, yadadamean.

**Husband:** She's the best in the Bay.

**Roach:** Best in the Bay? Then why your truck sitting on no hubcaps?

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** You stole her hubcaps?!

**Roach:** Stole? I relocated 'em. Service fee.

*(Tasha sprays him point-blank.)*

**Roach:** *(sniffing)* Mmm, lavender. Y'all trying to kill me or tuck me in?

*(laugh)*

**Husband:** Tasha, you gon' let him talk to you like that?

**Roach:** She ain't gon' do nothing. I'm her best client. Every time she leave, y'all call her back. I'm job security, mane.

*(laugh)*

**Loop:** Tasha writes up her bill, and the Roach adds a tip line for himself.

## E03 — Grandma's Got Gas

**Premise:** The Husband calls his mom, who swears she knows how to kill roaches. Turns out she's been buying from this one.

**Staging:** Living room. Grandma sits on the couch, joint in hand.

**Husband:** Mama, you said you know how to get rid of roaches.

**Grandma:** *(puffs)* Baby, I been killing roaches since before you was born.

**Roach:** Grandma! My favorite customer! You want the usual?

**Wife:** ...The usual?

*(laugh)*

**Grandma:** Hush. *(to the Roach)* How much for an eighth?

**Husband:** MAMA!

**Roach:** For you? Family discount. And I threw in a pre-roll 'cause your son hella bootsie.

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** She been buying from him this whole time?

**Grandma:** Where you think I been getting it? It slaps.

*(laugh)*

**Loop:** Grandma and the Roach share a lighter while the Husband buries his face in his hands and the Wife pats his back.

## E04 — Lease Violation

**Premise:** The couple brings in the landlord to evict the Roach. Turns out the Roach has been paying their rent with their own money.

**Staging:** Kitchen. Mr. Baines stands silently in the doorway with his clipboard and key ring. Exaggerated sitcom visit, not real tenancy advice.

**Husband:** Mr. Baines, you gotta evict him.

**Roach:** Evict me? Baines, tell 'em who been paying rent on this unit.

**Wife:** ...What?

**Roach:** Y'all late every month. I been sliding this man an envelope so y'all don't get put out. You welcome.

*(laugh)*

**Husband:** With what money?

**Roach:** YOUR money, dummy. I hit your wallet every first and fifteenth.

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** So he paid our rent... with our money... and we owe HIM?

**Roach:** Interest, lil mama. I'm a roach AND a loan shark. Twice the bite.

*(laugh)*

**Loop:** Mr. Baines shakes the Roach's tiny hand while the couple stares.

## E05 — Neighborhood Watch

**Premise:** Pearl starts a neighborhood watch to catch whoever's robbing the block. Everything traces back to the Roach and his Cousin.

**Staging:** Front porch. Pearl stands silently with a whistle and clipboard.

**Husband:** Pearl started a neighborhood watch. We on you now.

**Roach:** Neighborhood watch? I sold Pearl that watch, bruh.

*(Pearl looks at her wrist.)*

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** Somebody been stealing on this whole block. Packages, gas, Pearl's wind chimes.

**Roach:** Allegedly. The Cousin did the chimes. I just take a cut.

**Cousin:** *(popping up)* Y'all want a chime? Five dollars a ding.

*(laugh)*

**Husband:** That's it, we calling the police.

**Roach:** Go 'head. Who they gon' arrest, cuddy? You gon' point at the floor in court?

*(laugh)*

**Loop:** Pearl blows her whistle, and the Roach blows one back louder.

## E06 — The Lick

**Premise:** The couple throws a backyard BBQ to plan the final war with all their helpers. The Roach and the Cousin rob the cookout.

**Staging:** Backyard. The grill is smoking. Tasha, Mr. Baines, Pearl and Grandma sit at the table. The Roach stands on the gutter with a tiny megaphone.

**Husband:** Everybody's here. Tonight we plan how to end this roach.

**Roach:** This a stick-up! Everybody put the ribs on the ground!

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** Is he robbing our barbecue?

**Roach:** Robbing? This a meat tax, lil mama. Cousin, grab the links.

**Cousin:** *(snatching smoking hot links)* Ow, ow, ow, worth it!

*(laugh)*

**Husband:** Mama, do something!

**Grandma:** *(puffs)* Baby, them ribs was dry anyway.

*(laugh)*

**Roach:** Appreciate the cookout, bootsie. Next time season the meat. That was hella bland.

*(laugh)*

**Loop:** The Roach rides off on the Cousin's lawnmower, running on the couple's stolen gas, with a rack of ribs strapped to the back.

## E07 — Can't Get Rid of Him (finale)

**Premise:** All-out war. The couple and every helper raid the Roach at once. He still wins.

**Staging:** Living room. The Husband and Wife stand side by side. Tasha holds her sprayer, Pearl her whistle, and Mr. Baines an eviction notice. Grandma lights up in the back.

**Husband:** Everybody ready? On three.

**Wife:** I got your back, baby. Every time.

**Roach:** Aw, y'all brought the whole squad? That's cute. I brought the Cousin.

*(The Cousin waves, holding Tasha's hubcap.)*

*(laugh)*

**Husband:** This is OUR house!

**Roach:** Your house? I got a trap in your walls, a stash in your toaster, and your mama on speed dial. This MY house, bruh. Y'all just pay the bills.

*(laugh)*

**Wife:** Not after tonight. *(raises her slipper)*

*(Everyone charges. Smoke, whistle, spray, slipper. When it clears, they've pinned down... the Cousin in a cheap roach mask.)*

**Roach:** *(from the ceiling fan, counting cash)* Y'all really thought? I been dodging slippers since the '80s, mane. On my mama, the Roach runs this house.

**Husband:** BRUTUS!

*(Brutus lunges at the fan, barking. The Roach shrieks and climbs higher.)*

**Roach:** Not the dog! Not the dog, mane!

**Cousin:** Brutus, chill... chill... aight, half chill.

*(Brutus sits halfway down, still growling.)*

*(laugh)*

**Loop:** The ceiling fan spins the Roach in circles while Grandma waves up at him.
